# Sample Pack Notes

Official contest pack (`sample-pack.zip` → `sample-pack/`). Every fact below was checked with
`node scripts/inspect-pack.mjs sample-pack` (real type by magic bytes, SHA-256, pdfjs page count, encrypted/corrupt
status, text) and cross-checked with `pdftotext`. The image-only scan was inspected visually.
The resolution is recorded in `sample-pack/resolution.json` and built with `node scripts/build-package.mjs sample-pack`
(same logic as the app) → `output/T-2026-0417_Package.pdf`, verified by `scripts/verify-package.mjs`.

`order` is 1–10 in file order here, but the app always sorts by `order` itself.

## Hidden problems and how each is resolved
1. **Two trade licenses.** `trade_license_2025.pdf` expired on **2025-06-30**, before the deadline. `trade_license_2026.pdf` is valid until 2027-06-30. Use the 2026 one. If staff pick the 2025 one and enter its date, the app shows **Expired** and blocks generation.
2. **Identical-content duplicate.** `experience_cert.pdf` and `experience_cert (1).pdf` have the same SHA-256 (`91cb4ab6…`). The app flags "Same content as …" and will not put the copy into a second requirement. The copy is left out.
3. **Not a PDF.** `company_logo.png` is a real PNG (magic `89 50 4E 47`). It is rejected with a reason and can't be matched. It is also irrelevant to the tender.
4. **Misleading / opaque names.**
   - `scan_0042.pdf` is the **Signed Declaration (R10)**. It is an image-only scan with no text layer: signature and seal, "Tender T-2026-0417", dated 2026-10-15. Matching it needs the preview, not the name.
   - The filename numbers don't follow `order`: `01_financial_proposal.pdf` is order **9**, `02_technical_proposal.pdf` is order **8**, `03_tin…` is 2 and `04_vat…` is 3. The package follows `order`, not file names.
5. **Optional documents absent.** R06 (Audited Financial Statement) and R07 (Manufacturer's Authorization, which has an expiry) have no file. Both are **Not provided** and don't block generation. They are skipped in the package.
6. **Source PDFs print their own "Page 1 of 2 / 1 of 6" headers.** Our footer is `T-2026-0417 | Page X of 16` in its own 28 pt band. The verifier matches only our footer and checks that nothing else sits in the band.
7. **Mixed generators / sizes.** These are PDF 1.4 (text) and PDF 1.3 (scan, 171 KB). Each page keeps its size and orientation.
8. **No expired or deadline-day documents among the correct picks.** The only expiry trap is the 2025 trade license. Judge-mode coverage of the deadline-day and one-day-before cases is in `test-pack/` (see below).

## Judge-mode pack (`test-pack/`, generated by `scripts/make-test-pack.mjs`)
A separate, deliberately harder synthetic tender (`LGED-CTG-2026-1189`). It is used only to test that the rules hold on unseen data. It contains:
- unsorted `order` and alias keys (`deadline`, `required`, `expiry_check`)
- a document expiring exactly on the deadline, which must be OK
- an optional insurance expiring one day before, which must be Expired and blocking; the resolution excludes it
- a duplicate pair with different names
- a `.txt` renamed to `.pdf`
- a password-protected PDF
- an irrelevant newsletter
- landscape and `/Rotate 90` pages, and text at the very bottom edge

`npm test` runs both packs end-to-end.
